# Reliance Industries Q3 FY26 Financial Performance & Earnings Outlook

**Source Note**: [Synthetic / Hackathon Demo Content]

## Executive Summary
Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) delivered strong Q3 FY26 consolidated revenue growth driven by robust retail expansion and sustained ARPU gains in Jio Infocomm. Consolidated EBITDA grew 12.4% year-over-year to ₹44,200 crore.

## Segment Performance

### 1. Jio Platforms & Digital Services
- **Subscriber Base**: Reached 485 million subscribers with 5G penetration exceeding 38%.
- **ARPU Growth**: Average Revenue Per User increased to ₹198 per month, supported by premium tier upgrades and data usage acceleration.
- **EBITDA Margin**: Expanded to 51.2% reflecting operational efficiency.

### 2. Reliance Retail
- **Store Expansion**: Opened 420 new stores during the quarter, bringing total operational store footprint to over 18,800.
- **Footfalls**: Footfalls crossed 280 million across digital and physical touchpoints.

### 3. Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) & Energy
- **Throughput**: Sustained high operating rates with total crude throughput of 19.8 MMT.
- **Gross Refining Margin (GRM)**: Outperformed regional Singapore benchmarks due to heavy crude processing capabilities and optimized yield slate.

## Future Guidance & Earnings Outlook
Management reiterated capital expenditure discipline while accelerating green energy gigafactory commissioning in Dhirubhai Ambani Green Energy Giga Complex, Jamnagar.
